## 🎧 Recording Instructions for User

1. **Quiet Space:** Sit in a quiet room with minimal echo.
2. **Device:** Use your phone’s built-in Voice Recorder (iPhone Voice Memos / Samsung Voice Recorder) or WhatsApp voice note, or PC microphone.
3. **Pacing:** Speak at a calm, confident, conversational speed. Do not rush.
4. **Save File:** Save the recorded audio as `voiceover.mp3` or `voiceover.m4a` and drop it into `E:\Promptwars2026\`.
5. **Auto-Mux:** Once dropped, Antigravity will run `python scripts/mux_voiceover.py` to balance the voiceover with ducked background music and export `prana_ai_youtube_presentation.mp4`.
